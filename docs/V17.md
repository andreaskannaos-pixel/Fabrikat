# V17: endgame, rail, fluids, sea trade, rivers, campaign

All code is in `index.html`, in the block headed `V17`. Item and recipe data sit just before `const VAL=`, so every new item gets a price and depth from its recipe like everything else.

## Building-type registry (`BT`)

New buildings don't add more `switch` branches to the engine. Each one registers an object in `BT` with:

| Hook | Called from | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fp` | `footprint()` | size `[w, h]` (rail 1×1, lab, depot, boiler, port 2×2, turbine 3×2, silo 3×3) |
| `init(e)` | `mk()` | per-entity state (saved with the game automatically) |
| `accepts / give / take` | belts and inserters | logistics I/O |
| `tick(e)` | `tick()` over `L.v17` | simulation, adds to `powDem` through `drawKW` |
| `draw(g, e, tm)` | `drawEntity()` | also makes the build icon (the icon scale fits any footprint) |
| `panel(e)` / `act(e, k, v)` | `drawEnt()` / `entAct()` | UI and buttons (`data-act`) |
| `refund(e)` | `refund()` | returns stored items when removed |
| `warn(e)` | `warnOf()` | in-world warning icon with a hover explanation |
| `fluid` | `buildNets()`, `pipeLink()` | gets `e.fs`, the adjacent pipe networks, and pipes visibly connect |
| `place(x, y)`, `anyLand`, `walk` | `placeOk()`, `solidAt()` | coast rule, rails on neutral land, walkable tiles |

## Science labs

- `stats.labCur` is the technology being researched, and `stats.labProg[id]` counts finished units.
- A lab `tick`:
  1. If it holds one set of `r.sci` packs, it consumes them and starts a unit.
  2. It advances `powSat × (1 + 0.1 × exoLv) / 60` per tick.
  3. At `r.time` it calls `labUnitDone`, which finishes the technology when `units` is reached.
- Research entries with `sci` cost no money. The tech tree shows pack icons × units, a progress bar on the node, and Research in labs / Pause buttons (`data-lab`).
- **Exotic Industry** is `infinite`:
  - Each completion raises `stats.exoLv`, which multiplies `machSpd` by 1 + 0.1 × level.
  - Units needed = 200 × 1.5^level.
  - It stays locked until `stats.rocket`.

## Rocket silo

`IDLE → (parts in stock) → WORK (3 s per part, 1.2 MW) → READY (50 parts) → LAUNCH (600 ticks, drawn rising with an exhaust plume) → rocketLaunched()`

- The first launch sets `stats.rocket`, shows the victory screen, awards 500 credits and unlocks Exotic Industry.
- Later launches pay $2M.
- There is an auto-launch toggle.
- After the campaign, the mission card shows the endgame progress.

## Freight rail

- **Rails** are 1×1 entities. `rebuild()` writes them into `railMap` and bumps `railVer`.
  - They may sit on your land, on neutral land and on rivers (drawn with a wooden bridge deck).
  - They are never "off" and people can walk over them.
- **`railPath(a, b)`** runs a breadth-first search over `railMap`, from rail tiles touching depot `a` to rail tiles touching depot `b`.
  - It uses typed arrays with a stamp and is cached per depot pair and `railVer`.
- **Train state machine** (stored on the sending depot as `e.tr`):

```
LOAD (from depot inventory or its fluid buffer; leaves when full or 10 s after first cargo)
  → GO (accelerate 5 tiles/s², cruise 12 tiles/s, brake to arrive)
  → UNLOAD (1.5 s; waits with DEST FULL if the receiver has no room)
  → BACK (reversed path) → LOAD
Path broken while moving → cargo returns to the depot and the train resets.
```

- Capacity is 4 wagons: 400 items or 2,000 fluid.
- Fluid depots take from and put into adjacent pipe networks.
- Trains on shared track pass through each other. There are no signals or collisions.
- The old point-to-point train stations still work in existing saves.

## Fluids and power

- New fluids: `heavy_oil`, `light_oil`, `petgas`, `steam`. The cracking recipes are ordinary chemical-plant recipes, using the existing side-input and front-output pipe ports.
- **Boiler:**
  - It burns coal from an inserter, or fuel / heavy oil from a pipe.
  - It takes water from the adjacent water network and puts steam into a separate adjacent network (`steam` or empty).
  - Output is 12 steam/s from 6 water/s. Each steam unit carries 300 kJ, so one coal makes 60 steam.
- **Turbine:** `Turbines.run(need)` runs inside `powerTick`, after solar, geothermal and reactors and before the old steam generators.
  - Each turbine buffers steam and can give up to 1,800 kW.
  - Load is shared across turbines and consumes 6 steam/s at full load, so turbines only burn what the factory needs.
  - Spare capacity shows in the power readout.
- **Pressure** (`pressureSecond`):
  - `press = 20 + 280 × fill + 25 × pumps` kPa.
  - A network is `hungry` when a machine, boiler, turbine or generator waits for its fluid.
  - `starve = hungry && fill < 15%`. Starving pipes pulse red, and those machines show LOW PRESSURE.
  - Fluid behaviour is unchanged; this is a diagnostic readout.

## Sea ports and export deals

- A port is placed only where its footprint touches the sea.
- Its inventory takes any solid item, up to 2,000. Imported goods go to a separate store that inserters unload.
- `PARTNERS` are 4 overseas partners, each with a trip length, a price premium (25–60%), an optional rare-resource payment and a list of wanted items.
- **Ship cycle:**
  1. Docked, the ship checks the deals round-robin every second.
  2. It loads when a deal has at least 20 items above its "keep" amount (up to 300).
  3. It sails out (drawn to the nearest map edge) and sells at `VAL × premium × saturation`.
  4. It sails back and pays cash, or rare goods worth 115% of the cash price.
- Saturation: every sale adds to `stats.expSat[partner:item]`, which decays by 0.5% per second. Price = `max(0.5, 1 − saturation / 3000)`.

## Rivers

`genRivers` runs in `genMap` for worlds with `GENV ≥ 3` (new games). Older saves keep their map.

1. A breadth-first search gives every land tile its distance to the sea.
2. Up to 6 sources are chosen in mountain, volcanic, arctic or forest land, far from the sea and from HQ.
3. Each river walks downhill with noise-driven meanders and widens in its lower half.
4. Rivers stay at least 9 tiles from HQ.

Other rules:

- Deposits, village buildings and ruins avoid river tiles.
- Only rails, belts, pipes and wells can be placed on them.
- Wells get ×2 water on a river and ×1.5 within 2 tiles of one.
- Terrain chunks draw rivers with sandy banks.
