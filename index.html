Selling. Feed items into a Money Generator by belt, inserter or drill. Mk. I only takes raw ore; upgrade it to sell more advanced goods. Items an active contract needs go to the contract instead of being sold, and any generator will take them.
Moving items. Drills drop ore into whatever their chute faces. Belts feed any building they point at. Inserters move items from the building behind them into the one in front. Splitters share items between three outputs. Train stations ship cargo across the map.
Upkeep. Machines wear down as they work and slow down. Repair them by hand, or build a Maintenance Bay and feed it replacement parts.
