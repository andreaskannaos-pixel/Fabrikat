How things connect. A drill drops ore into whatever its chute faces: a belt, furnace or chest. Belts carry items into any building they point at. Inserters lift items out of the building behind them and drop them into the one in front (the arrow shows where). Furnaces burn coal, so feed them coal as well as ore. Assemblers craft whatever recipe you set. Click any building to look inside it.
