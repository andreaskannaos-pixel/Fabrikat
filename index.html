Selling. Feed items into the Money Generator by belt, inserter or drill. Mk. I only takes raw ore. Upgrade it to sell processed items, components and beyond. Every sale is a choice: sell iron plates now, or turn them into steel, then motors, which are worth far more.
Moving items. Drills drop ore into whatever their chute faces. Belts feed any building they point at. Inserters move items from the building behind them into the one in front. Furnaces burn coal. Assemblers, Chemical Plants and Fabricators make whatever recipe you set.
Land. You start with one plot. Buy neighbouring plots to reach oil, bauxite, titanium and the rare zones at the edge of the map.
